The Science of Photoperiod

The "photoperiod" is the total number of hours your lights are on. Too much light causes algae; too little light causes plant die-off.

Consistency is Key

Use a digital timer to ensure your lights turn on and off at the exact same time every day. Fluctuating light schedules are a leading cause of black beard algae (BBA).

The Algae "Siesta"

Some hobbyists use a "siesta" period—turning lights off for 1-2 hours in the middle of the day. This can help CO2 levels recover in low-tech tanks and disrupt the growth cycle of certain algae types.

Combine consistent lighting with regular water changes for a crystal-clear tank.
